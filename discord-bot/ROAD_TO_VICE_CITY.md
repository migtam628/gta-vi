## Community activity ideas

- **Trivia Tuesday:** Three short trivia questions from *existing* GTA games (known answers); members post answers, and host confirms later.
- **Screenshot Showcase:** Favorite official pre-launch screenshots and, after launch, in-game photos with spoiler warnings if appropriate.
- **Vice City Prediction Board:** Keep a running list of predictions and revisit them after launch; never portray predictions as confirmed details.
- **Role reward:** Give participants a `Vice City OG` role, with no paid entry or promised prizes.
- **Platform meetup:** Use `GTA 6 - PS5` and `GTA 6 - XBOX` voice channels for platform-specific help and conversation. Do **not** promise an unannounced GTA VI online multiplayer mode at launch.

## Moderation and sources

- Announcements should cite official Rockstar news rather than rumors.
- Official game hub: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI
- Official launch trailer: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer
- Official screenshots: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery
- Keep spoilers inside the spoiler channel, use `||spoiler||` tags, and avoid leaks or pirated content.
- Avoid `@everyone` for routine posts; one important reminder is usually enough.
- Keep bilingual messages readable, and let the multilingual countdown website handle longer translations.
